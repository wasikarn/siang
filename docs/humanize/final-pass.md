# Final pass (step 6 of the loop)

Run these in order on the draft from step 4, after resolving each step-5 bullet.

1. **Dash count.** Count the literal `—` in the prose you edited (cue #14) by re-running `scripts/scan.py` on the final; it prints the count. Dashes inside protected code, quotes or frontmatter stay and do not count. A claim that the dashes are cut is not a count of zero.
2. **Leftover tells.** Look once more for the four a first rewrite tends to leave: not-X-but-Y (§9), one-line closers (§60), forced triads (§10), bold labels (§15).
3. **Thai trailing clauses.** Read every sentence for a trailing `ถ้า`, `หาก`, `เมื่อ`, `หลัง`, `ตอน` or `ทุกครั้งที่` clause and move it to the front (§44). Cautious edits keep these because every word in them is correct.
4. **Source comparison.** Every fact, number, name and qualifier from the source is still there or was cut on purpose. In UI copy, a vague line that contradicts a specific status is cut (§36.1).
5. **Read aloud.** Vary sentence length. Confirm the step-2 grit landed in the text.
