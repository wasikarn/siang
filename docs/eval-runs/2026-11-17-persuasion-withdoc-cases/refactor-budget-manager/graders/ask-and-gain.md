Score 1 if the proposal makes one explicit request for a decision (approve 2 engineers for 2 weeks, or
similar) AND says what the manager or her director gains in their terms (fewer incidents or pages, faster
roadmap delivery after the refactor, or a way to measure the result). Score 0 if the request is implicit or
missing, or if the only benefit given is code quality for its own sake.
